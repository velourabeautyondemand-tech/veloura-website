# **App Name**: Nails On the Go

## Core Features:

- Technician Onboarding: Web form for technicians to apply, including license and details upload, followed by admin review and approval process.
- Mobile Booking: Customers can browse technicians who operate within a 6-mile service radius of their home base, view profiles, service menus, and book appointments via the mobile app.
- Automated Scheduling: System automatically blocks out travel time for technicians to prevent double-bookings.
- In-App Payments: Securely process payments at booking and after service completion, including tips and add-ons, via Stripe.
- Job Management: Technicians can manage availability, mark jobs in progress, request add-ons, and communicate with customers through in-app chat.
- Admin Dashboard: Admins manage service catalog, technician applications, bookings, payments, and generate reports through a web dashboard.
- Review Summarization: AI tool provides a summary of technician reviews, highlighting key positive and negative feedback.
- Browse & Book: Browse 15 predefined services, filter/search technicians within 6-mile radius, view technician profiles, and book appointments with various payment options.
- Cancellations & Rescheduling: Customer can reschedule up to 24 hours before appointment. Inside 24 hours, treated as no-show (no refund). If tech cancels, customer receives refund.
- Technician Jobs: List of upcoming & past jobs. Job details page (customer info, service, notes). Start job -> customer confirms start. Add-ons: request upsells during job -> customer approves. Finish job -> triggers payment capture.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Saturated blue (#29ABE2) to evoke trust and reliability in the on-demand service.
- Background color: Light blue (#E0F7FA) to provide a clean and airy feel.
- Accent color: Orange (#F47920) for CTAs, drawing the user's eye.
- Body and headline font: 'PT Sans' (sans-serif) for a modern and clean aesthetic.
- Use sharp, modern icons to represent service categories and app features.
- Mobile-first design with a clear, intuitive layout to facilitate easy booking and job management.
- Subtle animations and transitions to enhance user experience and provide feedback.

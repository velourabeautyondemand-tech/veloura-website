---

## 🐛 Found a Bug?

If you've encountered a bug while using our app or website, please let us know:

1. **Open an Issue** — Go to the **Issues** tab and click **"New Issue"**
2. **Describe the problem** — Include steps to reproduce, expected behavior, and screenshots if possible
3. **Label it** — Add the `bug` label to help us prioritize

---

## 💡 Have a Suggestion?

We're always looking for ways to improve the VÉLOURA experience:

1. **Open an Issue** — Go to the **Issues** tab and click **"New Issue"**
2. **Describe your idea** — Tell us what you'd like to see added or improved
3. **Label it** — Add the `enhancement` label

---

## 🔒 Security Concerns?

If you discover a security vulnerability, please **do not** open a public issue.

Instead, contact us directly at: **support@velourabeautyondemand.com**
